The Politics of Climate Change

Journal of Environmental Politics

Smith, J.; Müller, A.; Zhang, L.

Climate change is one of this century’s most significant challenges. Climate change mitigation and adaptation will require massive economic transformations and strongly affect other fields: infrastructure, health, agriculture, biodiversity, migration, and many more. How do political actors – parties, politicians, social movements, institutions, international organizations, etc. – react to and influence these transformations? And what about public opinion? Do citizens change their behavior (voting, consumption, etc.)? If yes, why and under what conditions? This paper introduces theories and methods on the nexus of climate change and politics. It aims to equip the reader with the skills to understand empirical research addressing party competition, political behavior, and policymaking.

Automated Data Collection with R

Political Science Research

Müller, A.; Schneider, F.

Against the background of the ever-increasing availability of (unstructured) online data, this paper introduces methods to collect large quantities of data for research projects, to process the data, and to automate this workflow in a reproducible way. It focuses on automated data collection using the R programming language. The paper illustrates three major modules: programming fundamentals, reproducible workflows (e.g., version control via GitHub), and static/dynamic web scraping for social science research.

Statistics

Working Paper Series, University of Innsbruck

Schneider, F.; Müller, A.

The paper provides knowledge of quantitative methods for investigating central questions in political science through practical examples. Its goal is to allow independent empirical analyses using R. Topics include univariate, bivariate, and introductory multivariate methods, as well as graphical and tabular presentation of results.

Data Collection and Analysis with R

Working Paper Series, University of Mannheim

Smith, J.; Zhang, L.

This paper introduces methods for collecting and analyzing web and social media data using the R programming language. Students are guided through programming basics, data collection, data processing, and analysis. The paper also addresses web scraping of social media platforms and traditional political communication data, illustrating practical applications of these methods.
